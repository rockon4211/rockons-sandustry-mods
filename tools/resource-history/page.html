Verdicts come from the map count over the selected range: SURPLUS = piling up, DEFICIT = draining, BALANCED = holding within ~1% (or 20 cells). "Your loop" is the average of what your Sources put in and Removers took out over the range; the gap between that and the map trend is what machines ate (or made). Shaded bands on the chart mark FAST-scan samples, which are estimates — EXACT counts are solid. With Game time selected, rates and the chart run on the mod's game clock, which stops while the game is paused, so a pause never reads as an hour of nothing changing.
